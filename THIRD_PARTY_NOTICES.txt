Radio Telescope Control - third-party components
=================================================

The program (main.py, control.py, mount_control.ino) was written for this project.
It does not contain code, icons or files from SDR#, Airspy or any SDR# plug-in.

The PC program (RadioTelescope.exe) contains only permissive-licensed components
(MIT / BSD / PSF). Keep this file, and the BSD notices of customtkinter, numpy,
astropy, pyserial and d3-celestial, with the product.

The firmware uses AccelStepper (GPL-3.0). When the telescope is sold with this
firmware on the Arduino, either provide the firmware source under the GPL, buy
a commercial AccelStepper license, or replace AccelStepper with own code.

Data are recorded with a separate SDR program chosen by the user (e.g. SDR#,
SDR++). Such programs are not part of this product and are not distributed with it.
